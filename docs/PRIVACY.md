# Privacy Policy

**sync-must-simple** is designed with privacy as its core principle. 

## Data Collection and Transmission
- **No Cloud Servers:** This extension does not transmit any of your data, reading habits, or browsing history to any cloud or third-party servers.
- **Local Network Only:** All synchronization occurs strictly within your Local Area Network (LAN) between your own devices, directly communicating with the local Go server you host.
- **Data Stored:** The only data tracked and stored is the scroll position (as a percentage), the URL of the specific domains you explicitly opt-in to sync, and a generated device identifier. 

## Permissions Explained
- **Storage:** Used to save your local server IP address and your list of opted-in domains locally on your browser.
- **Optional Permissions (Host Permissions):** The extension asks for permission to access pages *only* when you explicitly add a domain to your sync list. We do not request blanket `<all_urls>` permission upfront to ensure your browsing history on other sites remains completely private.
